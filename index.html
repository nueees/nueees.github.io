About

My name is Seeeun (three E’s — pronounced “Si-Eun,” though Germans always try to drop one). I’m a software engineer focused on using data to help teams work more effectively and efficiently.

I studied Culinary Food Management at university and worked as a restaurant manager. Eventually I quit that, learned to code, and became a developer. Along the way I've also worked at a diving shop and as a ski patrol.

After Covid19, I left Korea and settled down in Germany.

Fair warning: since I'm the one writing this, it's probably a bit polished with exaggerations, small white lies, and self-justifications. But I'll try to keep it real.

What I Do

I work with everyday data from teams - looking at how projects are running and figuring out what could be better. I make charts that show how team time is split up and where things are getting stuck. Basically, I watch what's happening and help find ways to make work faster and easier.
